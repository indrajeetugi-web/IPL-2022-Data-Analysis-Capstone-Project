# IPL-2022-Data-Analysis-Capstone-Project
A exploratory data analysis (EDA) of the IPL 2022 match-level dataset, built to derive meaningful insights into team dynamics, match outcomes, and individual player performances.

Key Insights Explored:
Match Performance: Evaluated team win distributions and overall success rates throughout the tournament.  
Toss Strategy: Analyzed toss decisions (bat/field trends) and their direct correlation with match outcomes.  
Player & Team Metrics: Tracked top scorers, high scores, first/second innings scores, and bowling figures across 74 matches.  

Technologies Used:
Python  Pandas & NumPy (Data Manipulation)  
Matplotlib & Seaborn (Data Visualization)
